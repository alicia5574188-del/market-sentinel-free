# Goal

## Active upgrade — 2026-09-07 range sweep/reclaim execution and adaptive risk

Stop treating a passive touch below a 15m range edge as a valid RANGE entry. A range-edge trade must first observe a liquidity sweep, then distinguish reclaim from outside acceptance with data the authority already owns, and enter only after either an exceptional real-time reclaim or a completed 1m reclaim followed by an economical boundary retest/reacceleration. Range positions must tolerate an ordinary wick outside the frozen boundary: the exchange-side hard stop sits beyond the observed sweep/structural/noise envelope, while evidence-based early exits require persistent completed-minute outside acceptance. Weak reclaim strength may reduce confidence or protection expectations but cannot by itself invalidate a still-reclaimed balance.

Raise the aggregate structural-risk hard ceiling from 5% to 10% for both PAPER and LIVE without increasing leverage-derived exposure. Preserve equity-proportional sizing and actual-fill recalculation, cap one trade at an adaptive 1.5%–3%, and add a stricter same-direction BTC/ETH/SOL correlated-risk ceiling so three nominally separate positions cannot consume the full 10% as one market bet. Existing open positions keep their frozen stops and risk. Keep LIVE default OFF, the owner-only control boundary, the 30% aggregate margin cap, the three-position limit, the 15-minute plan lifetime, the current Gate request/write budgets, and first-target-only economic admission.

Dynamic protection must not convert an ordinary pre-target pullback into a micro-profit exit. Before the frozen first liquidity target is reached, never trail a stop from a percentage of maximum favorable excursion and never move it beyond entry. Only after a completed 1m close has achieved both at least 1.5R and 70% of the actual first-target path may the remaining loss be reduced to 0.5R. The target itself remains a real-time exit/re-evaluation event; a confirmed node continuation may then adopt the next segment's structural invalidation. Existing already-tightened exchange stops are never widened during deployment.

## Active upgrade — 2026-09-07 breakout auction branching

Treat a boundary crossing as an auction event, not proof of continuation. Preserve the frozen parent/child 15m structure and branch one symbol lifecycle into exactly one of three outcomes: only exceptional real-time displacement may enter directly; an accepted but ordinary break must wait for a completed retest and renewed acceleration; a swept boundary that closes back inside must wait for an inside re-test and then trade toward the nearest reachable opposite-side liquidity. Uncertain crossings remain observation-only. Do not move the original boundary forward, chase an already-extended price, auto-reverse an open position, or let a farther 1h/4h node subsidize the actual first segment.

The implementation must use only data the current authority already owns: completed Gate 1m/15m/1h candles, locally aggregated 4h candles, fresh two-second books, microprice/OFI, rotating signed trades, OI, liquidations, and the existing per-symbol freshness gates. OI and liquidation evidence may strengthen a decision but cannot be mandatory every two seconds. PAPER and LIVE must consume the same frozen plan. Preserve adaptive 1.5%–3% per-entry risk, 10% aggregate and 6.5% same-direction structural risk, 30% aggregate margin, existing position protection, request/write budgets, owner-only LIVE control, and LIVE default OFF.

Replace the rolling twelve-candle "15m range" shortcut with an explicit parent/child 15m hierarchy. A child boundary break that remains inside the parent balance is an internal rotation toward the parent boundary, never a parent breakout. Admission economics must stand on the actual first target without borrowing a farther node. Preserve the existing PAPER/LIVE safety boundary, completed-candle rules, two-second internal breakout confirmation, 10% aggregate and 6.5% same-direction structural-risk caps, request/write budgets, and owner-controlled LIVE default-off behavior.

Build a clean Gate USDT futures system around one predictive question: which reachable liquidity concentration offers the best utility for the current segment? Each coin may expose several hierarchical soft routes at once—15m upper/lower breakout, range-edge rejection, and the later continuation beyond a 1h/4h liquidity node—but only one current segment may become an executable BREAKOUT, REVERSAL, RANGE, or WAIT lifecycle. The runtime must prepare before a boundary is crossed, reassess strength at every higher-timeframe liquidity node, use dynamic structural exits, and keep aggregate structural stop loss at or below 10% of the applicable PAPER or actual Gate equity with no more than 6.5% in one direction.

A PREPARED entry is one immutable lifecycle: later opposite-side scoring cannot replace it. Two-second flow may update the visible confidence but cannot veto a plan after that plan already passed the entry filter. A BREAKOUT wick touching the trigger is never enough to enter. Only an exceptional frozen route with at least 86% confirmation, at most 18% current fakeout risk, and four consecutive fresh two-second snapshots at least 0.1R beyond the trigger may execute directly; any broken sequence resets.
