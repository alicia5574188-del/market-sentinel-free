# Liquidity Three State

A deliberately small Gate USDT perpetuals system with one shared strategy and separate PAPER/owner-controlled LIVE execution. It maps several hierarchical liquidity routes per coin, then promotes exactly one current segment to `BREAKOUT`, `REVERSAL`, or `RANGE` execution.

## Runtime

- One SQLite Durable Object (`MarketStream`) owns the fixed BTC/ETH/SOL loop.
- Every two seconds it fetches three full futures order-book snapshots with IDs. There is no continuous WebSocket and no foreground market-data producer.
- Every five minutes, Gate futures metadata is refreshed for BTC, ETH, and SOL only. Other cycles pair three books with at most two ancillary requests: contract stats for OI plus rotating signed trades, public liquidations, or completed 1m/15m/1h structure candles. Completed 4h candles are aggregated locally from 1h rows, so higher-timeframe routing adds no Gate request.
- Completed 15m candles produce a parent balance and, when present, a nested child balance. Crossing the child while price remains inside the parent is an `INTERNAL_ROTATION` toward the parent edge, not a parent breakout. Only a pre-armed crossing of the parent boundary is a `LOCAL_BREAKOUT`; two completed closes consume an old boundary so rolling recalculation cannot revive it. Each frozen boundary crossing then branches into one auction outcome: an exceptional displacement may enter directly, an ordinary accepted break must retest and hold the original boundary before renewed acceleration, and a completed sweep-and-reclaim may prepare an opposite passive retest toward the nearest same-grade liquidity. While that route map exists, it is the only entry authority: the legacy all-timeframe fallback cannot create a distant order. A farther 1h/4h node is split behind a 0.65%–0.9% adaptive 15m measured objective; the system exits there unless fresh continuation strength promotes the next node.
- A 30-second compact checkpoint, immediate authority checkpoints for PAPER state changes, and a one-minute Cron watchdog recover eviction, stale alarms, 429s, and partial market outages. The hard budget is 43,200 alarm writes + 8,000 non-alarm writes + 2,880 watchdog reserve = 54,080/day.
- Actual chart candles are mirrored from the same authority to D1 at most once every five minutes; one continuously open operator page adds only cached D1 reads and no Durable Object request load. Each PAPER fill also keeps a bounded entry/exit review window and a close diagnostic in `paper_events`, so History can draw the real Gate 1m candles and exact entry/exit markers without replaying old market data. Every PAPER bankruptcy is retained there as a permanent account-level report.
- PAPER runs continuously. LIVE defaults off, requires the fixed `owner` account plus the existing `OWNER_ACCESS_TOKEN`, and can be changed only from an authenticated same-origin session. Its signed HttpOnly login lasts 30 days and renews on each authenticated page opening; login still never enables LIVE. The public runtime never returns balances, order IDs, positions, or credentials.
- LIVE reads the exact frozen PAPER plan. BREAKOUT is observed internally and becomes a current-price IOC only after the shared adaptive confirmation; REVERSAL and RANGE use resting Gate limit orders. Turning LIVE off cancels unfilled entries, while an already-open position keeps its exchange-side reduce-only structural stop and continues the same dynamic exit policy.
- PAPER and LIVE choose leverage from the same liquidation-aware function: approximately 10% margin per executable plan, no more than 30% aggregate pending/open margin, and no expansion of the existing 5% aggregate structural-risk budget.

## Decision and risk

`Score = P(reach) × (Liquidity + Cascade) ÷ (PathCost + DistanceCost) × Persistence`

Walls need 70% persistence across 30 snapshots and are removed when more than 50% cancel during approach. Liquidation levels are estimates built from entry-price-binned ΔOI cohorts, taker/price direction, contract multiplier, maintenance margin, 5x/10x/20x/50x leverage, opposing futures depth, and public liquidation calibration.

Prepared entries freeze their side, trigger, stop, target, and parent/child structure identity for up to 15 minutes. A breakout can be prepared only while price remains on the pre-trigger side. Its first crossing is recorded once; a return inside or a move beyond the 0.5R no-chase boundary cancels that lifecycle instead of allowing a later retrace to revive it. No later score or opposite-side recalculation can move or replace a valid plan, and one two-second score change cannot veto it at the exact trigger. Stale/sequence-fault data, a crossed frozen invalidation, a replaced structure identity, expiry, a passed target, or trigger-time risk/economics cancel immediately. Route/target disappearance, evidence below the lower hysteresis boundary, or movement away from the activation neighborhood must persist at two distinct completed 1m observations before cancellation. Targets that cannot cover the modeled 0.18% round-trip fee and stress-slippage friction are rejected before appearing on the order page.

Stops and exits are structural and dynamic. New stops must sit beyond both structural invalidation and the larger of modeled 0.18% round-trip friction or 1.1 times the recent 70th-percentile 1m candle range; widening this price distance automatically reduces size and never expands account risk. BREAKOUT uses one auction state machine shared by PAPER and LIVE. Direct entry is reserved for a frozen A-grade route with at least 86% confirmation, at most 18% fakeout risk, and four consecutive fresh two-second snapshots at least 0.1R but no more than 0.5R beyond the boundary. An ordinary completed 1m acceptance never enters by itself: a later completed 1m candle must approach the frozen boundary from outside, hold it, close directionally outside with at least 55% retention, and price must then re-cross that retest candle's extreme within 0.5R. A completed 1m sweep that closes back inside with reverse body/retention plus absorption and reverse flow instead prepares a passive inside retest in the opposite direction; it targets the nearest same-grade 15m liquidity before any farther edge or 1h/4h node. Uncertain crossings remain observation-only. RANGE and failed-break REVERSAL entries remain passive limits. Every segment's actual first target independently satisfies admission economics; a farther node is only a later decision context. After a structural stop, the same route and direction cannot re-arm until two completed 1m candles have elapsed and price has reclaimed the frozen trigger; a new structural route or opposite thesis remains eligible. The original structural stop executes immediately. Soft target/utility exits require at least two minutes in the trade, three distinct completed 1m confirmations of the same reason, and a completed close at least 0.75R adverse; a live two-second tick cannot trigger a soft exit. Dynamic protection updates only from a newly completed 1m candle: below 1R it cannot move; at 1R it may reduce remaining loss to 0.5R; after the favorable move covers round-trip friction plus another 0.5R it may cross entry, but must lock friction plus at least 0.15R net cushion before ratcheting gradually without widening. A BREAKOUT candle that reaches at least 2R but retains no more than 30% at its close is a completed-candle profit rejection and exits instead of returning all the way to the original stop. Original, dynamic-protection, and breakout-rejection exits have separate audit labels. Every entry must still have at least 1.2:1 net reward/risk after modeled 0.18% round-trip friction. PAPER and LIVE first share the same equity-proportional 1%–1.8% per-entry sizing, 4×-equity planned notional ceiling, and minimum 1.5%-of-equity net target-profit gate; at 1,000 U the modeled maximum round-trip friction is therefore 7.2 U, while an accepted target must offer at least 15 U net. LIVE then applies Gate's integer contract lot: if the proportional amount is below one indivisible lot, exactly one lot is allowed only when its actual margin, economics, and aggregate loss still fit the hard 5% account limit. An untradeable symbol is reported and skipped without disabling LIVE or blocking another affordable symbol. There is no PnL pause or fixed holding time. At 300 U PAPER equity, the authority closes only against fresh evidence, archives a complete bankruptcy report, and immediately begins a new 1,000 U cycle. The Durable Object owns both execution lanes; D1 is only an idempotent PAPER/review/account-log mirror and encrypted credential store.

LIVE is deliberately fail-closed: it refuses to start around an unrecognized Gate position/order or hedge mode, checkpoints intent before a mutation, reconciles uncertain submissions before retrying, and requests a reduce-only market close if protection cannot be created or tightened. The switch is never enabled by deployment or login.

## Verification

```bash
npm run test:direct
npm test
npm run typecheck
npm run lint
```

Ordinary production releases require the existing Cloudflare `OWNER_ACCESS_TOKEN` secret. The credential table is preserved, while the owner UI may hold either zero rows after an intentional delete or exactly the encrypted `live_exchange_credentials` row with `id=1` after save/replace. The release path never prints or rewrites either value.
