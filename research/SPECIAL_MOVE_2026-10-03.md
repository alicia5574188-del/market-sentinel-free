# Special-coin reconstruction — 2026-10-03

Owner scope: actively discover unusual liquid coins, retain their observations,
and participate in their actual explosive legs. Broad-market prediction no
longer permits or vetoes this controller's entries. This is a prospective
mechanical design; functional tests do not establish expected profitability.

## Discovery and continuous research

Use the existing15s multi-venue bulk ticker cycle. Bounded minute price snapshots
(1024coins ×64points, expire2h) produce observed15/30/60minute comparisons.
No interpolation or synthetic OHLC. Missing/gapped windows remain unknown.
During warmup the existing short impulse ranks candidates; it never proves entry.
Scan30 includes held/armed symbols, at most8 remembered active unusual names,
relative movers/nonresponders, and four rotating exploration names.

Deep research reuses continuous completed5m rows and existing1m rows. It retains
up to64 observations for6h across scanner rotation, bounded24KiB; held symbols
have priority. It reports15/30/45/60moves, group-weighted background, normal beta
and correlation, residual, actual recent turnover, watch phase and three causal
changes. Fresh quotes can reveal deviation before a5m close, but cannot create
structural proof. Normal beta/correlation are retained while a hypothesis is
unusual, so a historically correlated coin's sudden nonresponse is not erased
by the same event. Six hours is observation retention, not a promise of complete
six-hour tick history or permanent learned profitability.

Candidates include active nonresponse to a meaningful expected common move,
opposite movement, relative strength/weakness and own acceleration. Comparison
uses cluster medians, then a median across groups (at least3groups); unavailable
background is explicitly unknown and does not veto independent acceleration.

Recent activity requires the last3 completed5m bars' actual quote turnover from
one declared venue: total≥2500USDT, all positive, and at least25% of the prior12
bar median if that baseline is known. This complements unchanged Gate24h
execution-liquidity eligibility. Unknown recent turnover means no admission.
Bybit list[6], OKX list[7], KuCoin futures list[6] and Gate `sum` are preserved;
base volume/contracts are never relabeled USD. MEXC/HTX fallback volume units
remain unknown for this gate. A new burst can therefore wait for completed5m
turnover evidence; the system does not infer unobserved recent volume.

## Own-leg admission and execution

Being special is an observation, not a buy/sell instruction. The coin must have
an actual completed retained departure or pullback/restart beyond the reference
formed before the last3five-minute bars. Prefer existing continuous fresh1m
bars; otherwise use completed5m evidence. Three actual completed proof bars,
their immutable times/price, reference and stop form one event identity.
No new identity from repeated fresh ticks. Subsequent same-side events require
a completed counter-move and restart; following an actual exit the proof's
first bar must be after that exit, including after optional research eviction.

Admission independently checks own freshness, multi-source disagreement,
actual-side quote, stop distance≤3.5%, net reward/risk≥1.35, modeled cost space
and distance from proof. Target room is explicitly a volatility estimate,
not an observed guaranteed objective. Far late chases wait for a new restart.

Research supplies actual direction, score and geometry. The inherited response
step accepts this completed own proof as its thesis, then still requires new
fresh executable quote progress and support samples at its existing thresholds.
Old broad-market direction scores and generic30minute range position cannot
independently veto a verified own breakout; the own stop/cost/chase checks run
again at commitment. This avoids declaring a new controller while secretly
retaining the old direction gate. Ordinary15minute time-only same-side cooldown
is replaced by the stronger actual post-exit restart identity for this mode.

Fixed1000 allocation, existing portfolio risk/margin/minimum lot constraints,
shared native admission builder and PAPER preparation/submission/later observed
Gate-book matching remain unchanged. No account reset or forced validation order.

## Holding, memory and review

New special-route holdings use independent causal holding support, unchanged
initial hard stops, actual completed support break plus failed recovery, and
earned-profit defense under completed counter-pressure. A common permission
change alone is not an exit. No age exit or immediate reversal from a changed
rank. Old filled holdings retain their previous policy and immutable receipts.

Research is durable through the existing coalesced10s protection checkpoint;
financial changes keep their original immediate atomic path. Optional research
yields before critical financial protection, whose per-trade anchors/peaks remain
inside each actual trade. Snapshot/UI include observations, readiness/blockers,
own proof and actual holding/exit evidence even if no trade has happened.
No extra market requests/timers, scan/minute/execution/write budget increases,
LIVE switch mutation, private exchange verification or retrospective profit test.

Evaluate future natural snapshots for detection lead time, missed/late launches,
false nonresponse, active-coin coverage, exact blockers, actual fills, support
survival, exits, net cost and concentration. Nonresponse can resolve up, down or
remain flat. Quiet periods and passing tests do not demonstrate trading edge.
