# Original special signal, opposite actual entry

Owner requests new entries reverse the original `special-move-v1` signal.
`special-move-inverse-v1` preserves that controller's active unusual-coin
discovery, completed own-price proof, retest/restart admission and original
subsequent quote-response validation. Only a new completed source proof after
cutover can authorize an entry. The actual submitted direction is opposite.
This is entry inversion with actual-side management, not a second wallet or
mirroring the lifecycle of hypothetical source orders.

The original route remains immutable inside `sourceRoute`, including side,
proof bars/time/path, reference, initial protection and original room estimate.
At admission, the fresh BBO midpoint freezes `inverseAnchor`. Reflect the original
loss boundary around that anchor to obtain the actual initial loss boundary.
Native sizing still uses the existing fixed1000 allocation, applicable capital,
fee/risk, lot, isolated margin and shared PAPER/LIVE intent checks. Fresh actual
fill price must preserve the original source economics and stay within a bounded
drift from the frozen anchor. This prevents entry inversion from becoming an
unrestricted opposite-side market order.

The source protection price is retained only as a research reference. It is not
an actual take-profit forecast. Actual remaining-profit estimates are zero and
the actual winner plan has no fabricated target. Original completion and quote
response establish eligibility, not expected profit in the opposite direction.

New filled inverse orders use actual-side adaptive support/recovery and earned
profit protection. They do not use `event-response-v1` failed-launch exits, a
minimum holding timer, or the original source's hypothetical exit. Normal
pullbacks remain observable; hard risk boundaries, confirmed failed recovery,
new post-entry opposite evidence and earned-profit counter-pressure can exit
through the existing native queue. Short holding times remain possible when
those actual conditions occur. Never widen an existing protective boundary.

Already-filled orders preserve their controller and side. Account identity,
balances, history, manual LIVE intent and native transport remain unchanged.
Consume both the source signal ID and the actual intent ID; repeat same-side
entries require a genuine post-exit retest/restart rather than forgetting optional
research. No new request, timer, write lane or resource ceiling is introduced.

Execution and account views label inverse orders `原信号反向` and show original
versus actual side. Review exports include the immutable source route, actual
signal/fill prices and times, initial/current protection, adaptive holding
observations, actual fees and terminal settlement. Archived trades use the same
audit. Observational event research may continue for old orders but has no
authority over new inverse entries.

Functional verification compares original and inverse runs on identical
synthetic inputs, confirming equal original proofs/response/entry times and
opposite actual directions. It covers shared native admission, stale/drift and
malformed-proof rejection, duplicate/reentry rules, old-position continuity,
five-minute holding without failed-launch exits, actual hard protection,
lossless storage/overlay restoration, snapshots and manual-reset learning.
Original release gates, capacity tests and advancing read-only production
receipts are also required. These checks establish mechanics and accounting,
not a profitable backtest. A delayed historical loss during a protection-write
outage must not be reproduced or treated as guaranteed inverse profit.
