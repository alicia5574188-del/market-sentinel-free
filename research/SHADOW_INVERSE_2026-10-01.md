# Frozen source / inverse PAPER — authorized trial

## Exact decision authority
`lib/shadow-baseline` contains the complete decision engine, market opportunity engine and winner manager from published build `2b4fd60f77c9b78526bd5087940945fe7e86fab8`. Only relative import paths change. Its manifest pins those original and relocated bytes plus shared decision dependencies. The newly added integration in `9b8b3153` is not enabled on this source. Do not tune either direction during this trial.

The source alone selects symbols, side, entry timing, quantity, risk, partial exits, moving reference levels and final exits. All current operational data feeds/clocks are shared; after legacy drain only one signal engine runs. The source receives its own money, closed outcomes, consumed episodes and loss budgets, never the inverse wallet. It is seeded from the actually running account at cutover, not a fabricated historical checkpoint or a fresh hidden 1000 U deposit. Existing source exposure/history is seed state, excluded from paired performance. Pending just-retired integration entries are discarded and regenerated by the frozen policy, never relabeled as baseline decisions.

## Cutover and lifecycle
The original visible PAPER account epoch, money, history and equity curve remain. Visible pre-cutover holdings are managed close-only under their existing controller. They are not inverted, force-closed or recosted. Source seed holdings run the frozen source controller; their separate seed outcomes are not compared with new inverse trades. During drain there can temporarily be up to ten old visible holdings plus up to ten paired inverse holdings; the source's own ten-position limit is unchanged. Quote protection uses the union of held symbols within the unchanged 30-symbol capacity. These transition reservations are operational and not another source signal filter.

Only a source trade born after the committed cutover creates `iv-<sourceId>`. Quantity/contract multiplier are copied exactly, including absolute amounts of up to two partial reductions. A profitable source reduction must execute even if the inverse is losing. An inverse may not take independent profits, stop early, be sized by its own earnings, reopen on recovery, or switch to the profitable side. A source close of any reason ends the paired inverse. Reference price labels describe SOURCE exit triggers, not exchange-valid inverse stops.

Source transition, inverse receipts and account totals are committed atomically through the existing durable full-state transaction. Missing/future/stale event quotes cannot create a fictitious fill; a failed operation retains the last committed state. Revision/identity/remaining-quantity checks prevent duplicate fees/orders on restart or replay. Old shadow observations remain diagnostics only.

## Financial contract and limits
Both books use the existing 7 bp per-fill fee model and adverse 2 bp/day funding allowance. Source buys at ask/sells at bid; the inverse takes the opposite executable side of that SAME observed quote. Fee on OPEN is charged once; each reduced/closed quantity pays its own closing fee/funding once; one parent settlement includes all pieces and is counted once. `spreadDrag = -(sourceGross + inverseGross)` is explanatory attribution, NEVER another debit. Same-price theoretical net inversion is `-sourceNet - 2*sourceCost`; it is not the executable inverse curve.

This BBO PAPER model is not guaranteed depth/latency execution, signed actual funding, exchange-margin feasibility or liquidation simulation. Keeping exact inverse lifecycle can produce losses larger than the source risk budget; the source's initial stop is a PROFIT-side reference for the inverse. No independent risk action is silently added to improve the experimental result. For these reasons inverse PAPER rows are explicitly excluded from real-money owner/member publication in this trial; no owner switch or existing real holding is changed.

User-requested reset closes both books administratively, archives current records and starts the existing new account flow. If fresh prices are missing, administrative last-saved marks are explicitly labeled in fill receipts; they must not be counted as normal executable mirror observations. Deployment itself never requests a reset.

The difference is not retroactively credited. Candidate logs label their decision account and source build. Snapshots report retained pair coverage, missing records, administrative exits and bounded source-research observations; no claim of full historical tick replay.

The comparison samples once per minute in memory, retains at most 720 points including the origin, persists with existing writes, and explicitly breaks long gaps. No per-snapshot writes or new market requests, alarms, account namespaces, credentials or paid services. Hot source history uses the existing bounded history tiers and omits duplicate closed-position narrative text/PI; active evidence, source IDs, numerical entry/outcome/risk inputs remain intact; immutable paired financial receipts remain in standard archives. Source and inverse protection share the existing compact checkpoint, with separate direction-aware validation.
