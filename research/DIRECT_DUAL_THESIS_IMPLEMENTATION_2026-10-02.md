# Independent return / continuation execution

The prior release retained an active frozen-source and inverse-reference wallet.
This correction replaces that authority with a single actual PAPER account and
direct per-symbol research plans (`dual-thesis-v2`). Production invokes
`advanceDirectStrategy`; it does not invoke either companion account engine.

The market observer and original candidate response/location validation remain.
RETURN extracts the observed move's entry baseline, geometric stop, earned
plateaus, completed swing protection, persistent price failure and bounded
obstacle trims as memory on the actual return trade. These boundaries resolve
the return event; they are not an adverse-side hard stop. Intermediate loss
tolerance is retained. Actual own-side BBO, quantities and 5bp filled fees are
booked by the one account.

CONTINUATION can enter without a preceding losing RETURN. Causal adjacent
completed 5m acceptance or retest/restart, fresh per-symbol multi-venue pressure,
current own-side BBO, a known next obstacle and >=1.35 cost-adjusted reward/risk
must agree. It owns subsequent holding and exits. Confirmed continuation against
an existing RETURN ends that hypothesis; a separate new entry requires full
market readiness, current space and actual risk/margin capacity. All sunk losses
and switch costs remain booked. No market-wide automatic direction flip.

New quantities use1000 U allocation; current equity still bounds risk and
isolated margin. Native copies retain their established fixed capital anchor,
manual activation, quantity rounding, exactly-once sends, unresolved-close
fences and actual5x target. Existing open financial/native identities remain;
original paired receipts are preserved as inactive provenance. Ordinary
pre-trial legacy holdings drain under their existing rules. Previously committed
but unfilled exits remain obligations. No administrative reset or test trade.

Research, execution, orders and snapshots expose the actual chosen direction,
entry, holding and exit conditions. The active companion account/tab and
comparison disappear. Old frozen files remain byte-identical for offline tests.
Plans are bounded to30, actual positions to10; inactive books do not receive
new evaluations or protection overlays. Existing data requests, timers, write
priorities and storage limits remain. Quotes alone do not request full commits.
The2s evaluation clock stages one bounded protection overlay between10s durable
slots. Pending peaks/confirmation are restored before the next evaluation;
unsaved protection is not published as committed authority. Financial entry,
reduction and exit bypass the staging delay. Restart uses the last durable
overlay and its counter, without resetting the resource day or admitting an
early duplicate write. No extra timer, storage key or increased daily cap.

Synthetic mechanism, migration, restart, resource and real-Worker/fake-Gate
checks establish execution behavior. They do not establish future profitability,
85% participation, identical fills, or immunity from isolated liquidation.

Release must use reviewed PR/main and original Actions deployment, then confirm
exact build, unchanged current account/manual intent and advancing health and
financial persistence. Never publish private account research or credentials.
