# Relative Strength Breadth Cross Era V1

Research only. No production, PAPER, LIVE, credentials, or deployment behavior changes.

## First-principles hypothesis

Absolute momentum was not stable enough across eras. This experiment removes the contemporaneous market median first and trades only cross-sectional residual strength/weakness, then asks whether market structure can identify the subset where that relative signal is portable across eras.

Structure gates tested independently: market alignment, >=60% directional breadth, dual 24h/72h market trend, 24h+72h residual persistence, and a full alignment gate including BTC/ETH/SOL core direction.

## Anti-overfit protocol

Discovery uses only 2024-09..2025-08 plus 2025-09..2026-05. A rule must be stress-positive and adverse-entry-positive in both eras, PF >=1.03 in both, with >=7/12 and >=5/9 positive months. Frequency qualifiers are >=8 and >=12 independent decisions/day.

The 2026-06..2026-08 holdout is not evaluated at all unless at least one rule passes the predeclared discovery gate. Failed families therefore do not get rescued or retuned from holdout information.
