# Unified research plan — owner-approved 2026-10-01

## Scope
The owner approved a trial of graded research/execution integration and requested version-accurate diagnostics. Preserve the independent winner discovery proposal, normal pullback room, original hard-risk bounds, finite range-center plans and the existing partial-realization ledger. No account reset, history deletion, automatic LIVE toggle or real-money test. Keep 30-symbol analysis, existing 2s/5m clocks and data-source budgets.

## One plan, graded evidence
Newly generated plans keep `winner-preservation-v1` accounting compatibility and carry `researchVersion=research-plan-v1`. Plans without this tag retain their existing holding policy. Already armed plans are not relabeled. Side, event identity, entry geometry, initial failure boundary and range target are frozen; the research advice receipt is refreshed before execution, then its entry copy is retained on the trade.

Only fresh, confirmed, persistent adverse hypotheses affect new risk. Forming/weakening/expired/future observations cannot veto entries. Independently qualified trends keep the original response lane with a bounded risk haircut; ordinary candidates under confirmed opposing conditions use the existing additional 30s / four-support-sample confirmation. The actual receipt records which action and risk scale applied. Duplicate quote timestamps are not extra confirmation samples.

Market warnings never own a healthy position's exit. Price-derived RELATIVE/PATH/STRUCTURE labels count as one source group. A new active exit needs the existing adverse assessment plus actual post-entry price damage: a completed reference candle, a later break, then another completed close failing to recover (with current price still beyond the level and material risk/giveback), or the existing rapid-entry-falsification basis with a new independent adverse book receipt. Incomplete, pre-entry, noncontiguous or stale candles cannot substitute for that evidence. This may delay some exits; hard risk and previously established profit protection remain immediate. There is no time-only minimum hold or time-only full close.

Persistent market caution plus the position's own completed-candle deterioration can enter the existing causal partial-protection path. It cannot skip that path's profit gate, later-candle confirmation, minimum-lot/net-cost check, two-reduction maximum, or 35% remaining-runner floor. Healthy trend stop/trailing behavior is unchanged by a market warning alone. No separate research sell order exists. Unknown research is neutral context, never presumed opposition.

The execution page shows the frozen pending/holding plan area and actual protection level, not a current unrelated map. The old broad OHLCV map remains background and legacy support; it is not advertised as the current order's area or measured resting liquidity.

## Diagnostic contract
Snapshot root v2 is preserved for old clients, with `diagnosticVersion=research-plan-audit-v1`. Fingerprints now include winner, risk, hypothesis, reaction, lifecycle, realization and integration code. Cohorts separate current-entry, older-entry, unknown-entry, exits under current code and cross-version management. Missing records stay unknown. One parent trade includes all partial fills; partial realized profit is not remaining floating PnL.

Trade traces retain first market warning, first local concern, first protection, both partial realizations, observed peak and terminal action. Requested versus applied action and minimum-size reduction failures are distinct. Original plan/entry advice and terminal evidence keep timestamps and physical parameters; price aliases are not independent votes. Version spans are bounded. Detailed terminal evidence is retained at `trade.exitAudit` rather than duplicated inside the review attachment. Quote chatter yields first; the existing candidate journal remains 80KiB and prioritizes authorized paths. Optional audit bytes still yield before financial authority. Candidate coverage explicitly reports its bounded window and dropped records, not a fictitious full-market replay. Checkpoint coverage includes 45m and rejects future observations.

Normal exact-head release checks and a read-only advancing-state receipt are required before declaring rollout complete.
