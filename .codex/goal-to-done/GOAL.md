# Goal

Build a clean Gate USDT futures system around one predictive question: which reachable liquidity concentration offers the best utility for the current segment? Each coin may expose several hierarchical soft routes at once—15m upper/lower breakout, range-edge rejection, and the later continuation beyond a 1h/4h liquidity node—but only one current segment may become an executable BREAKOUT, REVERSAL, RANGE, or WAIT lifecycle. The runtime must prepare before a boundary is crossed, reassess strength at every higher-timeframe liquidity node, use dynamic structural exits, and keep aggregate structural stop loss at or below 5% of the applicable PAPER or actual Gate equity.

Keep the GitHub-to-Cloudflare release path and the AES-GCM/HKDF-compatible live credential format. The owner may verify, encrypt and save/replace row id=1 or intentionally delete it while LIVE is off and no managed exposure remains. LIVE must default off, require the fixed owner login and signed same-origin session, reuse the exact frozen strategy plans, and remain entirely controlled by the user. Never expose credentials or allow public mutations.

The operator page must be understandable without engineering knowledge: lead with the current action and the reason for entering or waiting; show PAPER data publicly and reveal real Gate equity, available margin, leverage, margin, entries, positions, and the LIVE switch only after owner authentication. Track only BTC, ETH, and SOL. Give each market its own freshness state, a compact staged-route list, and an actual Gate USDT futures candlestick chart with switchable 1m, 15m, 1h, and aggregated 4h strategy structure views. A chart must show only the active segment and local range boundaries; off-screen higher-timeframe destinations belong in compact badges so distant levels cannot flatten or clutter the candles.

During one open browser session, every main tab and nested Live view must retain its own last scroll position and selected controls independently. Navigating away and back must not reset charts, expanded details, API form state, or the selected Live subpage; a full close/reopen may reset UI state. The LIVE switch belongs in the Live page and Settings only, not in the global header.

A PREPARED entry is one immutable lifecycle: later opposite-side scoring cannot replace it. A fresh price crossing is evaluated against that frozen side only while its route and destination still exist, price remains in its activation neighborhood, trigger-time economics still provide at least 1.2 net R after modeled costs, local BREAKOUT confirmation still passes the fakeout filter, and REVERSAL/RANGE still show absorption. Every plan expires after 15 minutes and is automatically cancelled earlier when its data, route, target, or activation condition becomes invalid.

Every historical PAPER order must provide a real Gate USDT futures 1m review chart with exact entry and exit markers, planned stop/target, gross PnL, modeled cost, net PnL, holding duration, and a plain-language exit reason.

PAPER must never become permanently unusable after a drawdown. Preserve the current cycle until equity reaches 300 U; then safely close remaining PAPER exposure using fresh prices, permanently archive a detailed bankruptcy report, and start the next 1,000 U PAPER cycle automatically without pausing the 24-hour runtime. History must contain a dedicated Account Logs view with the current cycle and every archived bankruptcy, including a copyable machine-readable report for later Codex analysis.

PAPER and LIVE must use one shared proportional sizing policy: 1%–1.8% planned risk per entry before exchange rounding, no more than 4× account equity in planned notional, no more than 5% aggregate structural risk, and no entry whose modeled net target profit is below 1.5% of the applicable account equity. Dynamic leverage should target about 10% margin per actual plan while retaining roughly three structural-stop distances before estimated liquidation, respecting the exchange maximum, and keeping all pending/open margin at or below 30% of equity. LIVE rescales from actual Gate equity. If the proportional size is below Gate's indivisible one-contract lot, it may use exactly one contract only after recalculating actual margin, economics, liquidation distance, and the hard 5% aggregate loss boundary; otherwise that coin is visibly skipped without disabling LIVE or blocking other plans.
